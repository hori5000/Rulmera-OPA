## 3. 추적 커버리지 및 누락 자동 점검

현재 기준 **Leaf WBS 34개 모두 요구사항/수용기준/산출물/품질 ID가 연결**되어 있습니다. 아래 표는 이후 편집 과정에서 추적 필드가 빠진 WBS만 자동으로 표시합니다.

```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID
  wbs AS "WBS",
  file.link AS "작업",
  requirement_id AS "REQ",
  deliverable_id AS "DEL",
  quality_id AS "QA",
  assignee AS "담당"
FROM "20-Water-AI/00-프로젝트관리/WBS"
WHERE level = 3 AND (!requirement_id OR !requirement OR !acceptance_criteria OR !deliverable_id OR !quality_id)
SORT wbs ASC
```

> 표가 비어 있으면 현재 Leaf WBS에 필수 추적 필드 누락이 없다는 뜻입니다.

## 4. WBS 원본 자동표

```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID
  requirement_id AS "REQ",
  file.link AS "WBS/작업",
  requirement AS "요구사항",
  assignee AS "담당",
  assignee_org AS "소속",
  deliverable_id AS "DEL",
  quality_id AS "QA",
  verification_method AS "검증",
  status AS "상태"
FROM "20-Water-AI/00-프로젝트관리/WBS"
WHERE level = 3 AND requirement_id
SORT wbs ASC
```
